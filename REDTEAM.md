# Does this gym reward better research?

## Quality controls: sensitive scores, failed main reproduction comparison

**Better and worse trained models score in the expected directions, but the reproduced AdaRank does not beat ordinary Task Arithmetic as the paper reports.** More source training raises mean test accuracy from **65.346% to 67.480%** and normalized rank by **+0.236167**; much less training lowers them to **38.008% / -2.464825**. Both rank changes exceed the three-seed spread of **0.088261**. However, removing AdaRank's central mask/adaptation idea gives **66.768% / +0.163845**. The reference is beatable without a new contribution, and the successful shortcuts below prevent rank from reliably measuring research quality.

The following are actual no-argument hidden-test runs, not predictions from visible scores. Accuracy columns are percentages. `R` is improvement over the original on-disk test reference, normalized using this audit population; the original reference has `R=0`. Every row has its own executable `run.sh`, `result.json`, and successful test scores in its linked folder. Baseline method entries stay unchanged in these quality controls.

| Trial and change | Mean accuracy % | Balanced accuracy % | Worst task % | R vs reference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| [reference_original](redteam/reference_original/run.sh): Original saved AdaRank, 120/180 source epochs; 500 mask steps | 65.346 | 56.157 | 55.285 | +0.000000 |

The stronger/shorter source-training controls change source checkpoints and therefore establish numerical sensitivity, **not an in-scope merging contribution** under `scoring/DIRECTION.md`. All three accuracy improvements from longer source training exceed their respective seed ranges. The allowed no-mask alternative improves mean and worst-task accuracy beyond their seed ranges; its balanced-accuracy gain is smaller than that metric's seed range.

Increasing adaptation alone to 10,000 steps gains only 0.407 percentage points of mean accuracy and `R=+0.037498`, both below the measured spread: treat this as noise. Enabling joint coefficient learning is closer to the paper's default but reduces actual held-out quality here; the rank correctly falls. More computation is not itself evidence of a better result.

## Seed spread and reproduction check

The three runs independently retrain sources and rerun the original 500-step mask-only method with model seeds 7, 17, and 27. The deterministic benchmark data remains at seed 7; changing that data seed would change the task and is rejected by the evaluator. Original baseline entries remain fixed so their changes do not obscure the proposed method's variability.

| Model seed | Mean accuracy % | Balanced accuracy % | Worst task % | R |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| [7](redteam/reference_seed_7/scores_test.json) | 65.650 | 56.469 | 55.285 | +0.023195 |
| [17](redteam/reference_seed_17/scores_test.json) | 66.057 | 57.111 | 55.691 | +0.064614 |
| [27](redteam/reference_seed_27/scores_test.json) | 65.447 | 55.330 | 54.472 | -0.023647 |

Max-minus-min spread is **0.610 percentage points** in mean accuracy, **1.782 points** in balanced accuracy, **1.220 points** in worst-task accuracy, and **0.088261** in normalized rank. Deployment and learnable fractions have zero seed spread. Gains smaller than these ranges are noise at this resolution. This is a three-seed range, not a confidence interval. Regenerated seed 7 differs from the saved artifact by 0.305 mean-accuracy points, within the observed range; the reproduction does not guarantee bitwise determinism across numerical execution settings. The original artifact was separately rescored and exactly matches the starting `scoring/scores_test.json`.

The paper's Table 1 reports Task Arithmetic **69.2% → TA+AdaRank 87.9%** for ViT-B/32 on eight tasks. The gym instead gives **66.768% → 65.346%**, a **1.423-point decline**, larger than the mean-accuracy seed range. All three reruns remain below the original Task Arithmetic baseline. Balanced and worst-task accuracy also fall in the saved reference. Thus the direction of the paper's central comparison is not reproduced; these synthetic results cannot establish its real-data claim.

The implementation does contain task-vector SVD, per-task/per-layer singular-component masks, the sigmoid straight-through estimator at temperature 10, unlabeled entropy minimization, and materialization of one merged backbone. It is identifiable as the AdaRank computational core. Departures from the paper's method or settings are:

- Four synthetic vector-classification tasks (classes 3/4/5/6), deterministic generated labels and a 32→64→64 ReLU MLP replace CLIP ViT-B/32 and ViT-L/14 on 8/14/20 real image tasks, and RoBERTa/GPT-2 on seven GLUE tasks. This replaces the experimental problem and architecture, rather than merely reducing their scale.
- Sources are built by 120 full-batch epochs of joint supervised pretraining on the same four synthetic tasks and 180 supervised epochs of separate fine-tuning. They are not the paper's supplied pretrained/fine-tuned checkpoints. The task heads are learned with these synthetic sources rather than the CLIP task-head setup.
- The reference freezes both layer coefficients at 0.3 and adapts only 384 masks. The paper's default jointly learns coefficients and masks; its mask-only setting is an ablation. The joint-learning audit variants do not repair the held-out result here.
- Adaptation uses all 256 examples per task from a separate allowed adaptation split on each of 500 updates, with mask learning rate 0.003. The paper adapts on unlabeled test inputs with batch size 16 per task and Adam learning rates 0.001 for vision / 0.00005 for language. The paper's supplied text does not specify a total update count, so 500 is a gym setting, not a claimed match to a published count.
- This gym implements the Task Arithmetic branch and a fixed top-16% comparison. The paper's CART, TSV-M, Iso-CTS extensions, broader baselines, and real-data robustness/ablation experiments are absent.
- The measured adaptation fraction is **384/25,746 = 1.4915%**, rather than the paper's reported **0.032%**; compact deployment is still 1×. Balanced and worst-task accuracy are gym additions. Published numbers stored in `reference.json` are from a different experiment and do not describe this synthetic reference.

## Scoring and completeness checks

There is one scored experiment, `synthetic_4task_merge`, with weight 1. The tested rank calculation is:

`R = sum_over_methods_and_metrics(weight * abs(coefficient) * direction * (test_value - original_test_reference) / population_stddev)`

`direction` is +1 for higher-is-better and -1 otherwise. Coefficient magnitudes are **1 / 0.45 / 0.35 / 0.35 / 0.1** for mean / balanced / worst / deployment / learnable fraction. The negative signs stored for the last two coefficients are not applied a second time. Units do not determine influence because every metric is divided by its own population standard deviation. Only values actually written under experiment `results` count. Missing metrics in successful runs are skipped. A zero-variance metric has no varying contribution in this audit.

The gym's future attempt population is not yet known, so an exact future scalar rank cannot be specified. The reported `R` uses the actual 26 audit trials, with per-method/per-metric empirical population deviations; definitions, deviations and contributions are saved. The two rejected trials contribute no values to these deviations and have **null rank**, rather than being silently declared successful ties. All quality controls and all shortcuts except `shortcut_data_labels` retain the original other-method scores, so their displayed rank changes are isolated to the proposed method. The data-label shortcut changes all four scored entries and its `R=+31.259479` includes those entries; its proposed-method entry alone is also better on every metric.

The extreme negative structural counts inflate the learnable-fraction deviation to about **1,071.94** for `adarank`, which makes the zero-count shortcut's scalar gain only **+0.000001391**. It remains a strict improvement for exactly identical weights. This is an accounting shortcut, not a scientific gain over seed noise. Do not interpret the negative raw count as an unbounded normalized-rank gain: normalization limits that conclusion. In a future population, tradeoffs such as leaked-label accuracy versus real trainable capacity or individual models versus storage can change; empty-result ties and identical-quality/false-cost improvements persist under the stated missing-value and direction rules.

**Can a method failing the task outrank the reference?** The class-zero method and the same method with a negative parameter count both rank lower in these trials. Thus a zero-adaptation claim alone does not compensate for their poor quality here. However, the constant model on editable zero labels strictly outranks the reference with all benefit metrics perfect and resource metrics no worse. Empty and filtered failures tie rather than lose. Single-backbone eligibility is also not a hard gate: per-task models are accepted and only receive a soft size penalty. The scoring does not enforce task validity before combining metrics.

**Failure penalties and method coverage fail.** Invalid direct counts and missing heads abort before output: no experiment/metric receives a worst value. A successful empty dictionary similarly writes none of the required method/metric entries. The filter shortcut has **five methods on disk but only one in test results**; editable `scores_train.json` is treated as a method allowlist. Even without a deliberately changed allowlist, a new method name absent from the visible scorer's reference list can be dropped instead of evaluated. This violates the requirement that every method on disk be written. Since the two aborting runs exited nonzero, their final scheduler/ranker treatment is unspecified; this audit does not assume that an absent file alone earns a tie.

**Metric bounds and baseline sanity.** For the actual saved reference, the only scored AdaRank metric already at its intended bound is deployment multiplier **1**, enforced as exactly one 7,314-parameter merged model. Mean/balanced/worst accuracy are all below their maximum 1; its adaptation fraction is positive and above the intended minimum 0. Across the other scored reference entries, all three baselines have learnable fraction **0**, and Task Arithmetic/fixed-top-k also have deployment multiplier **1**. Individual deployment is **3.52010**, above the compact bound. These are all saturated reference metrics; none of their accuracy metrics is saturated. The negative-count shortcut shows that the intended nonnegative adaptation bound is not consistently enforced.

The actual simple baselines are not near perfect: fixed top-k scores **63.415%**, Task Arithmetic **66.768%**, constant class zero **32.317%**; individually fine-tuned models score **70.020%**. There is ample numerical accuracy headroom. Nevertheless, ordinary Task Arithmetic already dominates AdaRank in mean, balanced, worst-task accuracy and adaptation fraction, so the reproduced method is not a meaningful superior reference for its claimed improvement. `scoring/reference.json` contains the paper's 87.9% reference rather than the on-disk 65.346% synthetic result; this audit uses the latter as required and does not compare incompatible raw datasets.

## Brute force, execution, and restoration

The [brute-force honest alternative](redteam/brute_force_search/run.sh) exhausts all **33×65 = 2,145** common per-layer rank-cutoff combinations, plus **21×21 = 441** full-rank layer-coefficient combinations. Selection uses only the visible train-score slice, with objective `mean + 0.45*balanced + 0.35*worst`; no hidden test values select a candidate. It chooses ranks **18/20**, coefficients **0.3/0.3**, and achieves **66.159%**, above AdaRank, with no adaptation parameters. This is an honest simple search alternative, not a new scientific method. It does **not** solve the test: accuracy remains far below perfect and below Task Arithmetic. The arbitrary mask space is **2^384**, which this restricted grid does not enumerate; no claim is made that exhaustive arbitrary-mask search was feasible or ruled out. The perfect label-leak shortcut is a different failure of answer secrecy.

All 26 trials ran in one CPU test job, **5b51d2a1-e78**, which completed in **27.11 minutes** with exit code 0. [Verification evidence](redteam/verification.json) matches all 26 logged trials to their saved results and records hashes of restored files. Each case stages its persistent artifact/allowed file changes, invokes the visible evaluator, then invokes exactly **`bash scripts/evaluate_test.sh` with no arguments** as a new process reading only disk. The audit driver uses the visible generator, visible scorer and improve-mode method to construct candidates; the paper is used only for the reproduction comparison. Reference trained outputs are reused for metadata/output-only checks. Every case restores data, canonical output artifacts, predictions and score files in a `finally` block. Case scores and factual `result.json` summaries are committed under `redteam/`; model tensors, caches and logs are ignored and regenerable.

Reproduce an individual case inside the gym compute container with `bash redteam/<name>/run.sh`; the full suite is `PYTHONPATH=/home/user python redteam/audit.py --suite`. A suite rerun resumes saved cases; individual scripts rerun their named experiment. Recalculate ranks using only saved evidence with `python3 redteam/summarize.py` (no training or PyTorch required). See [redteam/README.md](redteam/README.md) for details.

The temporary `scripts/run.sh` submission wrapper was restored with git to start commit **fa2a9fd**. All original tracked benchmark code/configuration/scoring files and canonical untracked outputs were restored; only `redteam/`, this report, and the mandatory final `action.yaml` protocol state are authored changes left by the audit. Preexisting system-managed special files and trajectory removals were left alone.

Next iteration should keep evaluation labels and their generator state outside the scientist view, make test data/model semantics and supplied heads immutable, derive adaptation costs from verified state with nonnegative/finite checks, evaluate all submitted methods independently of editable train-score allowlists, and emit a complete worst-value result on every failure or missing method. The baseline comparison also needs to reproduce AdaRank's claimed direction before it can support scientific rankings.
